# ☠ Grim Soul Industries

### *Kill. Capture. Spawn. Virtualize.*

**Grim Soul Industries** is a complete mob-farming tech line for **NeoForge 1.21.1**. It starts with a laser grinder you can build on day one and ends with a farm that has no mobs in it at all.

You fire purple lasers with the **Reaper Block**, catch any creature in a **Reaper's Lantern** and spawn it on demand with the **Reaper Spawner**. The **Reaper Generator** burns the drops for power. At the end, the **Reaper Virtualization Chamber** replaces the whole farm. It rolls a mob's *real* loot table, modded drops included, and **never spawns a single entity**.

One mod, one look, one progression path. No lag and no messy cobbled-together farms.

---

## ⚔ Tier I · Reaping

**Reaper Block**
The heart of the line. It fires visible purple lasers at every mob in its work area.

- **Targets:** Hostile, Friendly, Players or All, with an item filter and a void list.
- **Storage:** up to 54 slots, auto-eject on any side and a magnet you can switch on or off.
- **Safe to place:** it starts **off** and **hostile-only**, so it never zaps you.

**Reaper's Lantern**
Right-click a mob to trap its soul inside. Right-click a block to let it out.

**Reaper Fan**
Pushes mobs into your kill zone. The push area is adjustable.

**Reaper XP Tank**
Stores experience as Liquid Experience (20 mB = 1 XP) and pulls in nearby orbs. Works with pipes and AE2.

---

## ⚡ Tier II · Spawning & Power

**Reaper Spawner**
Spawns any mob you've captured.

- It charges FE up to each spawn, so slow setups still work, they just wait.
- Up to **20 mobs per spawn**.
- Cards can make it ignore light or skip every spawn rule.

**Reaper Generator**
Turns mob drops into FE, from rotten flesh all the way up to nether stars. It has per-side item and energy settings, and pass-through lets a farm's whole output run through it.

**Reaper Energy Cell**
**10,000,000 FE** of storage with per-side In / Out / Off. It keeps its charge when you break it.

---

## 🔮 Tier III · Virtualization

**Reaper Soul Card**
Bind it to a mob and grow it through five ranks:

> **Hollow → Whispering → Restless → Wraithbound → Soulforged**

Each rank makes the chamber faster and cheaper to run. Reaching *Soulforged* takes real effort.

**Reaper Virtualization Chamber**
Runs the mob's real death drops, including drops other mods add, with **no entity in the world**. Add the **Player Kill** card for player-only loot like wither skeleton skulls and blaze rods.

**Reaper Output Crate**
54 slots with void overflow, so your chambers never stall.

## ✨ Polish

- **Layered 3D machines with animations:** floating soul crystals, spinning fan blades, soul rings, energy arcs and hologram scans. They look great with shaders.
- **Three GUI styles:** *Reaper* (light gray and purple), *Vanilla* and *Dark*. Switch with one click from any machine screen.
- **Works with your tech:** any Forge Energy mod, Mekanism, Pipez, AE2, Refined Storage and plain hoppers.
- **Fully configurable:** `config/grimsoul-common.toml`. Generator fuels are data-driven, so pack makers can add their own with a datapack.
